## phoenix_diagrams

![PhoenixDiagrams screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oshanz/phoenix_diagrams/refs/heads/main/docs/screenshot.png)

A Phoenix library that keeps a searchable catalog of Mermaid and PlantUML diagrams inside your app, rendered client-side with pan/zoom, copy-to-clipboard, image export, and live reload in development.

- **Language:** Elixir
- **Package:** [hex.pm/packages/phoenix_diagrams](https://hex.pm/packages/phoenix_diagrams)
- **Source:** [github.com/oshanz/phoenix_diagrams](https://github.com/oshanz/phoenix_diagrams)

## disposable_email

An open-source Elixir library for blocking disposable email signups. It validates email addresses against a continuously updated list of known disposable email providers, helping applications prevent throwaway accounts at registration.

- **Language:** Elixir
- **Package:** [hex.pm/packages/disposable_email](https://hex.pm/packages/disposable_email)
- **Source:** [github.com/oshanz/disposable-email](https://github.com/oshanz/disposable-email)
